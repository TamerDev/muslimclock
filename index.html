Prayer alerts you can check, with no ads and no tracking.

Muslim Clock is a free iPhone app for prayer times, Athan alerts, the Qibla direction, a monthly timetable and widgets. Prayer times are calculated on your iPhone, and the app collects no personal data that identifies you.

Everything you need to pray on time is free. Optional Muslim Clock Plus adds a Tasbih counter, prayer performance charts, widgets, extra themes and timetable sharing.

Support: help with alerts, prayer times, Qibla and widgets.
