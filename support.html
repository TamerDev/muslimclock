Prayer alerts

My Athan alert didn't go off

Open the menu in the app and choose Alert health. It shows whether notifications are allowed and how far ahead alerts are scheduled. You can also tap Send a test alert in 10 seconds.

If alerts still don't arrive, check:

  • Notifications are on: iPhone Settings › Notifications › Muslim Clock.
  • Focus or Do Not Disturb: a Focus can silence alerts. Allow Muslim Clock in that Focus.
  • Silent mode: alert sounds follow your iPhone's ring/silent switch.
  • Open the app every week or so. iOS limits how many alerts an app can schedule ahead, so the app plans about the next 10 days of alerts. Opening it tops them up.

The alert plays only part of the Athan

iOS allows notification sounds of up to 30 seconds, so alerts use a short clip. Tap the alert to hear the full Athan in the app, with a Stop button.

Prayer times

The times don't match my mosque

Mosques use different calculation methods, and many add a few minutes. In the app's Settings you can change the Calculation method, the Asr calculation method and the High latitude rule. Under Time adjustments you can move any prayer by up to 30 minutes either way.

The times are for the wrong city

Go to Settings › Change or refresh location. You can use your current location or search for a city. This also works when location access is off.

The Hijri date is a day off

Moon sighting differs between countries. Use Date adjustment in Settings to move the Hijri date by up to 2 days.

Qibla

Hold your iPhone flat and away from metal, magnets and other electronics. If the app asks you to calibrate, move your iPhone in a figure-8 until the compass settles.

Widgets

Open Muslim Clock once after installing it and set your location, then add the widget from your Home Screen or Lock Screen. Widgets use the same settings as the app and update by themselves at each prayer time.

Language

Choose English, Arabic or Urdu in Settings › App language.

Privacy

Muslim Clock collects no personal data that identifies you. It sends anonymous usage counts, which you can turn off in Settings › Share anonymous usage data. Read the Privacy Policy.
