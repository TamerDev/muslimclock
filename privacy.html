Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

In short: Muslim Clock does not collect personal data that identifies you. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your location and your prayer history never reach us. To improve the app, it counts how often features are used, anonymously, and you can turn that off.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

To work, the app stores these things on your device only:

  • your location (coordinates, city name and time zone)
  • your prayer settings, such as the calculation method, time adjustments and date adjustment
  • your alert choices, voice, language and theme

This data is shared with the Muslim Clock widgets on the same iPhone, so they can show your prayer times. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Location

Muslim Clock asks for your location only while you are using the app, never in the background. It uses your location on your iPhone to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. If you prefer not to share your location, you can search for your city instead.

Apple services the app uses

Two features use Apple's services, which are covered by Apple's Privacy Policy:

  • Showing your city name. The app asks Apple to turn your coordinates into a place name.
  • City search. When you search for a city, the text you type is sent to Apple Maps to find matching places.

We never see these requests or their results.

Anonymous usage counts

To learn which features are useful, Muslim Clock sends anonymous counts to Google Firebase Analytics, for example "the app was opened", "a prayer was marked as prayed", "the Performance screen was opened" or "an Athan voice was changed". Each count carries at most one simple detail, such as which of the five prayers, which voice, or which Plus plan.

Never sent: your name, email, location or coordinates, city, time zone, contacts, or the history of which prayers you marked on which days. We do not set a user ID, we do not use the advertising identifier, and we do not ask to track you across other apps.

As with any analytics service, Firebase gives each installation a random identifier and works out an approximate region from your internet connection; we have turned off Google's more detailed location and device data. We see only totals, such as how many people used a feature this week, never an individual's activity.

To turn it off: in Muslim Clock, open Settings › Share anonymous usage data. When it is off, nothing is sent. Firebase is covered by Google's Firebase privacy information.

Purchases

Muslim Clock Plus is sold through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. The app checks your purchase with Apple on your iPhone.

Notifications

Prayer alerts are scheduled on your iPhone. We do not run a notification server, and alerts are not sent over the internet.

Links to other websites

Some screens link to outside sources, such as the full text of a verse on quran.com. These open in your browser only when you tap them, and that website's own privacy policy applies.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash reports. They do not identify you. You can turn this off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Children

Muslim Clock does not collect personal data that identifies anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
